# Commercial Operations

Field force, targeting, alignment, CRM, incentive compensation and customer master.

# Concepts

* [Call Plan](/commercial-operations/call-plan.md) - Target list × planned frequency per cycle for each territory.
* [CRM for Life Sciences](/commercial-operations/crm.md) - System recording accounts, calls, samples, approved emails and events (Veeva CRM/Vault CRM, Salesforce Life Sciences Cloud, IQVIA OCE).
* [Customer Master Data Management (MDM)](/commercial-operations/customer-master-mdm.md) - Golden HCP/HCO records built by matching NPPES, OneKey, Veeva, AMA, DEA/HIN and source keys; every sales/claims feed is mastered to it.
* [Field Reimbursement Manager](/commercial-operations/field-reimbursement-manager.md) - Supports offices with benefit verification, PA and coding within Anti-Kickback limits.
* [HCO Hierarchy](/commercial-operations/hco-hierarchy.md) - System (IDN) → organisation → site/department hierarchy; in Veeva-mastered data, system VID → organisation VID.
* [Incentive Compensation (IC)](/commercial-operations/incentive-compensation.md) - Rep payout computed from goals and credited sales (867/DDD, Xponent, SP) via alignment rules.
* [Key Account Manager](/commercial-operations/kam.md) - Manages IDNs, health systems and large accounts (contracts, P&T, pathways).
* [Medical Science Liaison (MSL)](/commercial-operations/msl.md) - Non-promotional scientific role engaging KOLs and answering unsolicited medical questions; separate from sales.
* [Omnichannel Engagement & Next Best Action](/commercial-operations/omnichannel.md) - Coordinated rep, email, web and media touches with model-driven next-best-action suggestions.
* [Prescriber & Ship-to Matching](/commercial-operations/prescriber-matching.md) - Mapping vendor IDs (IMS ID, DEA, HIN, custom keys) to master VIDs/NPIs; the unmatched rate is a key data-quality KPI.
* [Sales Force Sizing](/commercial-operations/sales-force-sizing.md) - Rep count = targets × frequency ÷ capacity, tested against response curves.
* [Pharmaceutical Sales Representative](/commercial-operations/sales-rep.md) - Field employee who delivers on-label product messages, samples and access information to HCPs and accounts; measured on calls, reach/frequency and territory sales.
* [Sampling & Sample Accountability](/commercial-operations/sampling.md) - Distribution of free samples under PDMA with signed requests, lot tracking and reconciliation.
* [Targeting, Segmentation & Deciling](/commercial-operations/targeting-segmentation.md) - Ranks HCPs/accounts into deciles by market volume (Rx, claims, sales) and potential to decide who reps call on.
* [Territory Design & Alignment](/commercial-operations/territory-alignment.md) - Assignment of geographies (zips) and accounts to territories, balancing workload and potential; usually realigned annually.
