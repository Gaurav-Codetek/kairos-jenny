# Market Access & Patient Services

Payer access, hubs, copay and specialty distribution design.

# Concepts

* [Copay Accumulators & Maximizers](/market-access/copay-accumulators.md) - Plan designs where assistance doesn't count toward deductible/OOP or is spread to exhaust programme maximums.
* [Copay Assistance](/market-access/copay-assistance.md) - Manufacturer cards reducing commercial patients' out-of-pocket cost; excluded for federal programmes under the AKS.
* [Hub Services](/market-access/hub-services.md) - Manufacturer-funded patient services: benefit verification, PA support, financial assistance triage, SP routing, adherence.
* [Patient Assistance Program (PAP)](/market-access/patient-assistance-program.md) - Free drug for uninsured/underinsured patients via a foundation or manufacturer programme.
* [Payer Contracting & Pull-Through](/market-access/payer-contracting.md) - Negotiating formulary position for rebates, then ensuring field teams communicate the win.
* [Specialty Pharmacy Network Design](/market-access/sp-network-design.md) - Open, limited or exclusive SP networks with data and service agreements.
