# Entities

Logical entities with identifiers and grain.

# Concepts

* [Call](/data-model/entities/call.md) - CRM interaction.
* [Chargeback](/data-model/entities/chargeback.md) - 844 line.
* [Contract](/data-model/entities/contract.md) - Pricing agreement.
* [HCO](/data-model/entities/hco.md) - Organisation/account (dim_hco).
* [HCP](/data-model/entities/hcp.md) - Individual prescriber.
* [Medical Claim](/data-model/entities/medical-claim.md) - 837 claim and lines.
* [Patient](/data-model/entities/patient.md) - De-identified patient.
* [Payer / Plan](/data-model/entities/payer-plan.md) - Benefit plan.
* [Transfer of Value](/data-model/entities/payment.md) - Open Payments record.
* [PBM](/data-model/entities/pbm.md) - Pharmacy benefit manager.
* [Pharmacy](/data-model/entities/pharmacy.md) - Dispensing location.
* [Pharmacy Claim](/data-model/entities/pharmacy-claim.md) - NCPDP transaction / final fill.
* [Prescription](/data-model/entities/prescription.md) - Order and its fills.
* [Product](/data-model/entities/product.md) - Drug package and its hierarchy.
* [Rebate](/data-model/entities/rebate.md) - Rebate invoice line.
* [Rep](/data-model/entities/rep.md) - Field user.
* [Sample](/data-model/entities/sample.md) - Sample disbursement.
* [Shipment / Sale Line](/data-model/entities/shipment.md) - 867 or ex-factory line.
* [Territory](/data-model/entities/territory.md) - Sales geography (effective-dated).
