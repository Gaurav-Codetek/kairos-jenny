# FDA Pathways & Programs

Approval pathways and FDA oversight programmes.

# Concepts

* [21 CFR Part 11](/regulation/fda/21-cfr-part-11.md) - Requirements for electronic records and signatures (CRM sample signatures, eTMF).
* [ANDA 505(j)](/regulation/fda/anda.md) - Abbreviated application for generics proving bioequivalence.
* [Biosimilar 351(k)](/regulation/fda/biosimilar-351k.md) - Abbreviated pathway for biosimilar or interchangeable biologics.
* [BLA 351(a)](/regulation/fda/bla.md) - Biologics License Application for novel biologics.
* [Clinical Trial Phases](/regulation/fda/clinical-trial-phases.md) - Phase 1 safety, Phase 2 dose/efficacy, Phase 3 pivotal, Phase 4 post-marketing.
* [Investigational New Drug (IND)](/regulation/fda/ind.md) - Application required before first-in-human trials.
* [NDA 505(b)(1)](/regulation/fda/nda-505b1.md) - Full New Drug Application with the sponsor's own safety/efficacy data.
* [NDA 505(b)(2)](/regulation/fda/nda-505b2.md) - NDA relying partly on literature or prior FDA findings (new forms, combinations).
* [NDC 12-Digit Final Rule](/regulation/fda/ndc-12-rule.md) - FDA rule moving the NDC to a uniform 12-digit 6-4-2 format.
* [Off-Label Promotion Restrictions](/regulation/fda/off-label-promotion.md) - Manufacturers may not promote unapproved uses; MSLs may answer unsolicited requests.
* [OPDP & Promotional Rules](/regulation/fda/opdp-promotion.md) - FDA Office of Prescription Drug Promotion reviews Form 2253 submissions; promotion must be consistent with the label.
* [Pharmacovigilance & FAERS](/regulation/fda/pharmacovigilance.md) - Adverse-event collection and reporting (15-day expedited reports) into FDA FAERS; reps must forward AEs heard in calls.
* [REMS](/regulation/fda/rems.md) - Risk Evaluation and Mitigation Strategy; may restrict prescribing/dispensing (ETASU).
