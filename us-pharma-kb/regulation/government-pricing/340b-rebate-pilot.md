# Definition
HRSA pilot letting manufacturers pay 340B discounts as post-sale rebates; vacated Feb 2026, relaunched Aug 2026 for MFP-selected drugs.[^hrsa-340b-pilot]

Planned start 1 Jan 2027, limited to NDC-11s subject to a Maximum Fair Price. Not in effect at time of writing.
