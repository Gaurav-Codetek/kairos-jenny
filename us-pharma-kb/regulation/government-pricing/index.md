# Government Pricing Programs

Statutory price and rebate programmes.

# Concepts

* [340B Drug Pricing Program](/regulation/government-pricing/340b.md) - Covered entities buy outpatient drugs at the ceiling price (AMP − URA), mostly via wholesaler chargebacks.
* [340B Rebate Model Pilot](/regulation/government-pricing/340b-rebate-pilot.md) - HRSA pilot letting manufacturers pay 340B discounts as post-sale rebates; vacated Feb 2026, relaunched Aug 2026 for MFP-selected drugs.
* [Medicaid Drug Rebate Program](/regulation/government-pricing/mdrp.md) - Manufacturers pay states quarterly rebates (URA × units) based on AMP and Best Price; cap removed from 2024.
