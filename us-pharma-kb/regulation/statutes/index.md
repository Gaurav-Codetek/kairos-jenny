# Statutes

Core federal statutes.

# Concepts

* [Biologics Price Competition and Innovation Act](/regulation/statutes/bpcia.md) - 2010 law creating the 351(k) biosimilar/interchangeable pathway and 12-year reference exclusivity.
* [Controlled Substances Act](/regulation/statutes/controlled-substances-act.md) - Establishes Schedules I–V, DEA registration, EPCS and ARCOS reporting.
* [Drug Supply Chain Security Act](/regulation/statutes/dscsa.md) - 2013 law requiring package-level serialisation and interoperable electronic tracing between trading partners.
* [Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act](/regulation/statutes/fdca.md) - 1938 statute governing drug safety, efficacy, approval, labeling and misbranding.
* [Hatch-Waxman Act](/regulation/statutes/hatch-waxman.md) - 1984 law creating the ANDA generic pathway, Orange Book patent listing, Paragraph IV challenges and 180-day exclusivity.
* [Inflation Reduction Act (drug provisions)](/regulation/statutes/ira.md) - 2022 law creating Medicare drug price negotiation (MFP), Part D redesign, the Manufacturer Discount Program and inflation rebates.
* [Prescription Drug Marketing Act](/regulation/statutes/pdma.md) - 1987 law governing drug samples: written requests, signed receipts, inventory reconciliation.
