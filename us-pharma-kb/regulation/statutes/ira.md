# Definition
2022 law creating Medicare drug price negotiation (MFP), Part D redesign, the Manufacturer Discount Program and inflation rebates.[^kff-negotiation]

MFPs took effect for 10 drugs in 2026; 15 more Part D drugs (incl. semaglutide) take effect 1 Jan 2027; 15 Part B/D drugs were selected in 2026 for 2028.
