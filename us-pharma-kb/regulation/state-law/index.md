# State Law

State-level rules affecting commercial data.

# Concepts

* [AMA Physician Data Restriction Program](/regulation/state-law/ama-pdrp.md) - Lets physicians opt out of reps seeing their prescribing data; surfaced as a PDRP opt-out flag in Rx and claims feeds.
* [Sorrell v. IMS Health (2011)](/regulation/state-law/sorrell-v-ims.md) - Supreme Court struck down Vermont's ban on using prescriber-identifiable data for marketing, keeping prescriber-level Rx data legal.
* [State Marketing & Disclosure Laws](/regulation/state-law/state-marketing-laws.md) - State gift bans, spend disclosure (e.g. VT, MA, MN) and rep licensing.
