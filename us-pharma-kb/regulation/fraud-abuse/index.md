# Fraud & Abuse / Transparency

Kickback, false claims and transparency rules.

# Concepts

* [Anti-Kickback Statute](/regulation/fraud-abuse/anti-kickback-statute.md) - Criminal prohibition on remuneration to induce federally reimbursed business; reason copay cards exclude government-insured patients.
* [False Claims Act](/regulation/fraud-abuse/false-claims-act.md) - Civil liability (with qui tam whistleblowers) for causing false federal claims; basis of off-label and kickback settlements.
* [PhRMA Code on Interactions with HCPs](/regulation/fraud-abuse/phrma-code.md) - Voluntary industry code on meals, speaker programmes and gifts.
* [Stark Law](/regulation/fraud-abuse/stark-law.md) - Prohibits physician self-referral for designated health services.
* [Physician Payments Sunshine Act (Open Payments)](/regulation/fraud-abuse/sunshine-act.md) - Requires manufacturers/GPOs to report transfers of value to physicians, NPPs and teaching hospitals.
