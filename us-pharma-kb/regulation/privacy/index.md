# Privacy

HIPAA and de-identification.

# Concepts

* [HIPAA De-identification](/regulation/privacy/hipaa-deidentification.md) - PHI is de-identified by Safe Harbor (remove 18 identifiers) or Expert Determination (very small re-identification risk); the basis for tokenised claims data.
* [HIPAA Privacy Rule](/regulation/privacy/hipaa-privacy-rule.md) - Governs use and disclosure of PHI by covered entities and business associates.
* [Limited Data Set](/regulation/privacy/limited-data-set.md) - PHI with direct identifiers removed but dates/zip retained, usable under a Data Use Agreement.
