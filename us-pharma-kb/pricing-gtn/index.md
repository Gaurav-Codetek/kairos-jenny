# Pricing & Gross-to-Net

List prices, statutory prices, discounts and the GTN waterfall.

# Concepts

* [340B Ceiling Price](/pricing-gtn/340b-ceiling-price.md) - AMP − URA per unit.
* [AMP (Average Manufacturer Price)](/pricing-gtn/amp.md) - Average price paid by retail-community wholesalers/pharmacies; base of Medicaid and 340B.
* [ASP (Average Sales Price)](/pricing-gtn/asp.md) - Quarterly net-price average reported to CMS; Part B pays ASP + 6%.
* [AWP (Average Wholesale Price)](/pricing-gtn/awp.md) - Compendia benchmark (≈ WAC × 1.2) still used in PBM pricing.
* [Best Price](/pricing-gtn/best-price.md) - Lowest price to any eligible commercial customer; drives Medicaid URA.
* [Chargeback](/pricing-gtn/chargeback.md) - Wholesaler claim for WAC minus the customer's contract price (GPO, IDN, 340B, FSS).
* [Commercial / PBM Rebate](/pricing-gtn/commercial-rebate.md) - Rebates and admin fees paid to PBMs/plans on utilisation invoices for formulary position.
* [Copay Program Cost](/pricing-gtn/copay-cost.md) - Manufacturer spend on copay assistance.
* [Distribution Service Fees](/pricing-gtn/distribution-fees.md) - Fee-for-service paid to wholesalers and SPs.
* [FSS / Federal Ceiling Price](/pricing-gtn/fss-fcp.md) - VA Federal Supply Schedule and Big Four ceiling prices.
* [Gross-to-Net (GTN)](/pricing-gtn/gross-to-net.md) - WAC × units minus chargebacks, rebates, government discounts, copay, fees, returns and prompt pay = net revenue.
* [Medicaid Rebate](/pricing-gtn/medicaid-rebate.md) - URA × utilisation units invoiced quarterly by states.
* [MFP (Maximum Fair Price)](/pricing-gtn/mfp.md) - IRA-negotiated Medicare price, effectuated through the Medicare Transaction Facilitator.
* [Part D Manufacturer Discount](/pricing-gtn/part-d-discount.md) - 10% (initial) / 20% (catastrophic) discounts under the IRA Manufacturer Discount Program.
* [URA (Unit Rebate Amount)](/pricing-gtn/ura.md) - Medicaid rebate per unit = basic (23.1% of AMP or AMP − BP) + CPI-U inflation penalty.
* [WAC (Wholesale Acquisition Cost)](/pricing-gtn/wac.md) - Manufacturer list price to wholesalers before discounts (warehouse: `WAC`, `WAC_DOLLARS`).
