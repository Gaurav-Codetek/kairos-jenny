# Reference Data

Master lists used to interpret transactions.

# Concepts

* [Drug Compendia (FDB, Medi-Span)](/reference-data/drug-compendia.md) - Commercial drug files with WAC/AWP history, packaging, GPI/GCN and clinical attributes.
* [Market Basket Definition](/reference-data/market-basket.md) - The set of competitor and own products defining a market for share calculations (warehouse: `market_basket_treatments`).
* [FDA NDC Directory](/reference-data/ndc-directory.md) - Daily-updated list of listed NDCs and package descriptions.
* [NDC–HCPCS Crosswalk](/reference-data/ndc-hcpcs-crosswalk.md) - CMS ASP crosswalk mapping J-codes to NDCs with billing-unit conversion.
* [FDA Orange Book](/reference-data/orange-book.md) - Approved drugs with therapeutic-equivalence codes, patents and exclusivities.
* [Product Master](/reference-data/product-master.md) - Internal hierarchy brand → molecule → form → strength → NDC-9 → NDC-11 with market-basket membership.
* [FDA Purple Book](/reference-data/purple-book.md) - Licensed biologics, biosimilars and interchangeables with exclusivity data.
* [Specialty & Profession Codes](/reference-data/specialty-codes.md) - Vendor (IQVIA/AMA) and NUCC specialty codes used for HCP segmentation.
