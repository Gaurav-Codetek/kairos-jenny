# PBMs

Pharmacy benefit managers.

# Concepts

* [CVS Caremark](/actors/pbms/cvs-caremark.md) - CVS Health's PBM.
* [Express Scripts (Cigna/Evernorth)](/actors/pbms/express-scripts.md) - Largest US PBM by claims share in 2025.
* [Optum Rx](/actors/pbms/optum-rx.md) - UnitedHealth Group's PBM.
* [Pharmacy Benefit Manager (PBM)](/actors/pbms/pbm.md) - Adjudicates pharmacy claims, builds formularies, contracts pharmacy networks and negotiates manufacturer rebates for plan sponsors.
