# Distribution

3PLs, wholesalers, specialty distributors and GPOs.

# Concepts

* [Third-Party Logistics Provider (3PL)](/actors/distribution/3pl.md) - Warehouses manufacturer-owned inventory and ships orders to wholesalers and direct accounts; often also runs order-to-cash and sends 867 sales data.
* [Full-Line Wholesaler](/actors/distribution/full-line-wholesaler.md) - Buys from manufacturers at WAC and supplies pharmacies, hospitals and clinics; the Big Three are McKesson, Cencora and Cardinal Health.
* [Group Purchasing Organization (GPO)](/actors/distribution/gpo.md) - Negotiates contract prices on behalf of hospitals, IDNs and clinics; its contracts drive wholesaler chargebacks.
* [ICS (Integrated Commercialization Solutions, Cencora)](/actors/distribution/ics-cencora.md) - Cencora's North American 3PL brand; ships manufacturer product and delivers ICS 867 sales/returns data and analytics.
* [Specialty Distributor](/actors/distribution/specialty-distributor.md) - Distributor serving physician offices, infusion centres and hospitals with physician-administered and specialty drugs (buy-and-bill).
