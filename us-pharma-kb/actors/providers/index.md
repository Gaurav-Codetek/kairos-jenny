# Providers

HCPs, HCOs, IDNs and covered entities.

# Concepts

* [340B Covered Entity](/actors/providers/340b-covered-entity.md) - HRSA-registered hospital or grantee entitled to 340B ceiling prices.
* [Dialysis Provider](/actors/providers/dialysis-provider.md) - Dialysis organisation (e.g. large dialysis chains) administering IV drugs such as iron; hemodialysis volume is often separated in IV iron analytics.
* [Healthcare Organization (HCO)](/actors/providers/hco.md) - Hospital, clinic, infusion centre, dialysis unit, practice or other organisation that buys, administers or bills drugs.
* [Healthcare Professional (HCP)](/actors/providers/hcp.md) - Individual prescriber or clinician (physician, NP, PA, etc.).
* [Integrated Delivery Network (IDN)](/actors/providers/idn.md) - Health system owning multiple hospitals and practices; the top of the HCO hierarchy (the system-level ID in Veeva-mastered data).
