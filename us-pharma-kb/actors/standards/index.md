# Standards Bodies

Organisations maintaining data standards.

# Concepts

* [HL7](/actors/standards/hl7.md) - Health Level Seven: HL7 v2 and FHIR clinical/financial interoperability standards.
* [NCPDP](/actors/standards/ncpdp.md) - National Council for Prescription Drug Programs: Telecommunication D.0, SCRIPT, ePA, provider IDs.
* [ASC X12](/actors/standards/x12.md) - Accredited Standards Committee X12: EDI 837/835/270/271/278 and supply-chain 850/856/867/852/844/849.
