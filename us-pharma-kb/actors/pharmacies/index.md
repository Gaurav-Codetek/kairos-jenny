# Pharmacies

Dispensing channels.

# Concepts

* [340B Contract Pharmacy](/actors/pharmacies/340b-contract-pharmacy.md) - Pharmacy dispensing 340B-priced drug on behalf of a covered entity.
* [Long-Term Care Pharmacy](/actors/pharmacies/ltc-pharmacy.md) - Serves nursing homes and assisted-living facilities.
* [Mail-Order Pharmacy](/actors/pharmacies/mail-order-pharmacy.md) - Central-fill pharmacy, usually PBM-owned, shipping maintenance drugs.
* [Retail Pharmacy](/actors/pharmacies/retail-pharmacy.md) - Chain or independent community pharmacy dispensing outpatient prescriptions and submitting NCPDP claims.
* [Specialty Pharmacy (SP)](/actors/pharmacies/specialty-pharmacy.md) - Dispenses high-cost/complex drugs with clinical support; sends status and dispense data to manufacturers under data agreements.
