# Payers

Commercial and government payers.

# Concepts

* [Commercial Payer](/actors/payers/commercial-payer.md) - Employer or individual-market health plan; the only channel eligible for manufacturer copay cards.
* [Medicaid](/actors/payers/medicaid.md) - State-federal programme; outpatient drugs carry statutory MDRP rebates.
* [Medicare Part B](/actors/payers/medicare-part-b.md) - Medical benefit covering physician-administered drugs, paid at ASP + 6% under buy-and-bill.
* [Medicare Part D](/actors/payers/medicare-part-d.md) - Outpatient drug benefit for Medicare beneficiaries delivered by private PDP/MA-PD plans.
* [VA / DoD / PHS](/actors/payers/va-dod.md) - Federal purchasers buying at FSS / Federal Ceiling Price.
