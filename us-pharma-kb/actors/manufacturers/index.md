# Manufacturers

Companies holding approvals and selling drugs.

# Concepts

* [Biosimilar Manufacturer](/actors/manufacturers/biosimilar-manufacturer.md) - Holder of a 351(k) BLA for a biosimilar or interchangeable biologic.
* [Brand Manufacturer](/actors/manufacturers/brand-manufacturer.md) - Company holding an NDA/BLA that markets branded drugs, sets WAC, contracts with PBMs/GPOs and funds patient programmes.
* [Generic Manufacturer](/actors/manufacturers/generic-manufacturer.md) - Holder of an ANDA selling bioequivalent copies after patent/exclusivity expiry.
* [Labeler](/actors/manufacturers/labeler.md) - Any firm named by an NDC labeler code: manufacturer, repackager, relabeler or private-label distributor.
