# Regulators

Federal and state agencies.

# Concepts

* [CMS](/actors/regulators/cms.md) - Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services: Medicare/Medicaid, NPPES, Open Payments, ASP/AMP reporting, IRA negotiation.
* [DEA](/actors/regulators/dea.md) - Drug Enforcement Administration: controlled-substance scheduling, registrations, EPCS, ARCOS.
* [FDA](/actors/regulators/fda.md) - Food and Drug Administration: approvals (CDER/CBER), labeling, promotion (OPDP), safety, NDC listing, DSCSA.
* [HHS-OIG](/actors/regulators/hhs-oig.md) - HHS Office of Inspector General: Anti-Kickback enforcement, advisory opinions, fraud alerts.
* [HRSA Office of Pharmacy Affairs](/actors/regulators/hrsa-opa.md) - Administers the 340B Drug Pricing Program and OPAIS registry.
* [State Boards of Pharmacy](/actors/regulators/state-boards.md) - License pharmacies, wholesalers and 3PLs; run PDMPs.
