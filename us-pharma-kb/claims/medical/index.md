# Medical Claims

X12 837/835 claims adjudicated by health plans.

# Concepts

* [Buy-and-Bill](/claims/medical/buy-and-bill.md) - Practice buys drug from a specialty distributor, administers it, and bills the J-code on the medical benefit (Part B pays ASP+6%).
* [ICD-10-CM, CPT, HCPCS, POS](/claims/medical/code-sets.md) - Diagnosis (ICD-10-CM), procedure (CPT/HCPCS), place-of-service and revenue code systems on medical claims.
* [Medical Claim Flow](/claims/medical/medical-claim-flow.md) - Provider → 837P/837I → clearinghouse → payer adjudication → 835 remittance; 270/271 eligibility, 276/277 status, 278 PA.
* [White / Brown / Clear Bagging](/claims/medical/white-brown-bagging.md) - Alternatives to buy-and-bill where an SP ships patient-specific drug to the provider (white), the patient carries it (brown) or a health-system SP supplies it (clear).
* [X12 835 Remittance](/claims/medical/x12-835.md) - Payment/remittance advice with claim- and line-level paid amounts and CARC/RARC adjustment codes.
* [X12 837I (Institutional)](/claims/medical/x12-837i.md) - Electronic UB-04: type of bill, revenue codes, ICD-10-PCS, DRG, attending NPI.
* [X12 837P (Professional)](/claims/medical/x12-837p.md) - Electronic CMS-1500: billing/rendering NPI, ICD-10-CM diagnoses, CPT/HCPCS lines with units, modifiers, place of service and NDC (N4).
