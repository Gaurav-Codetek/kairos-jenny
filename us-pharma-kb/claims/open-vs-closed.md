# Definition
Closed claims come from payers and are complete for enrolled members; open claims come from switches/clearinghouses with broad but incomplete coverage.[^komodo-method]

Komodo notes closed sources are typically available about one month after the claim; open-source lag ranges from daily to weekly. Use closed claims for persistence/incidence denominators; open claims for breadth and recency.

[^komodo-method]: [Komodo Health: standard methodology](https://www.komodohealth.com/reports-methodology/)
