# Analytics & KPIs

Standard commercial metrics and analyses.

# Concepts

* [Adherence (PDC / MPR)](/analytics-kpis/adherence-pdc.md) - Proportion of days covered or medication possession ratio over a period.
* [Approval, Rejection & Abandonment Rates](/analytics-kpis/approval-rejection-rates.md) - Initial vs final claim outcomes from lifecycle claims (LAAD/FIA).
* [Channel Inventory / Days on Hand](/analytics-kpis/channel-inventory.md) - Ex-factory sell-in minus 867 sell-through, or 852 on-hand ÷ daily demand.
* [Forecasting](/analytics-kpis/forecasting.md) - Patient- or trend-based demand and revenue projections.
* [GTN Rate](/analytics-kpis/gtn-rate.md) - Total deductions ÷ gross sales.
* [Launch Tracking](/analytics-kpis/launch-tracking.md) - Early indicators for new brands: trial prescribers, NBRx, access, time to therapy.
* [Lines of Therapy](/analytics-kpis/lines-of-therapy.md) - Sequencing of regimens per patient using claims rules (gap, add-on, switch).
* [Loss of Exclusivity & Generic Erosion](/analytics-kpis/loe-erosion.md) - Volume and price decline after generic/biosimilar entry.
* [Market Share](/analytics-kpis/market-share.md) - Brand TRx/NRx/NBRx, units or $ divided by the market basket total for the same dataset and period.
* [Patient Journey](/analytics-kpis/patient-journey.md) - Diagnosis → referral → Rx → PA → dispense/administration → persistence, built from linked claims and hub/SP data.
* [Payer Mix](/analytics-kpis/payer-mix.md) - Distribution of a brand's volume by channel (commercial, Part D, Medicaid, cash).
* [Persistence](/analytics-kpis/persistence.md) - Share of patients still on therapy N days after start (allowing a gap), from patient-level claims.
* [Promotion Response](/analytics-kpis/promotion-response.md) - Statistical lift in Rx/sales attributable to calls, samples and digital touches.
* [Reach & Frequency](/analytics-kpis/reach-frequency.md) - Share of targets called and average calls per target per period, from CRM.
* [Time to Therapy](/analytics-kpis/time-to-therapy.md) - Days from referral/written date to first dispense or administration.
