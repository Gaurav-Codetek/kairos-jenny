# Prescription Metrics

NRx, TRx, NBRx and unit measures.

# Concepts

* [Extended Units / Volume](/prescriptions/metrics/extended-units.md) - Dispensed quantity in units (tablets, mL, vials); used for unit-based share and for injectables.
* [NBRx (New-to-Brand Rx)](/prescriptions/metrics/nbrx.md) - Prescriptions for patients new to the brand (new starts, switches, add-ons); needs patient-level data such as LAAD `NEW_TO_BRAND_YN`.
* [NRx (New Prescriptions)](/prescriptions/metrics/nrx.md) - Prescriptions with fill number 0 (definitions vary by vendor).
* [Source of Business](/prescriptions/metrics/source-of-business.md) - Classification of a brand Rx as new start, switch, add-on, continuing or restart (LAAD `SOURCE_OF_BUSINESS`).
* [TRx (Total Prescriptions)](/prescriptions/metrics/trx.md) - New prescriptions plus refills.
