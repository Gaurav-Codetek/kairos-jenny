# Prescriptions

How prescriptions are written, transmitted, approved, dispensed and recorded.

# Sections

* [Prescription Metrics](/prescriptions/metrics/index.md) - NRx, TRx, NBRx and unit measures.

# Concepts

* [Prescription Abandonment & Reversal](/prescriptions/abandonment.md) - An approved claim not picked up is reversed (B2); abandonment rate = reversed / (paid + reversed).
* [Dispensing & Adjudication](/prescriptions/dispensing.md) - Pharmacy selects NDC, quantity, days supply and fill number, submits a real-time claim and dispenses on approval.
* [E-Prescribing](/prescriptions/e-prescribing.md) - Electronic transmission of prescriptions from EHR to pharmacy using NCPDP SCRIPT over a network such as Surescripts.
* [EPCS](/prescriptions/epcs.md) - DEA-compliant e-prescribing of controlled substances requiring two-factor authentication.
* [Formulary, Tiers & Utilization Management](/prescriptions/formulary.md) - Plan list of covered drugs with tiers, exclusions, step therapy and quantity limits.
* [NCPDP SCRIPT](/prescriptions/ncpdp-script.md) - Standard for NewRx, RxRenewal, RxChange, CancelRx, RxFill and ePA messages.
* [Prescription Drug Monitoring Program](/prescriptions/pdmp.md) - State database of dispensed controlled substances, reported in ASAP format.
* [Prescription](/prescriptions/prescription.md) - A prescriber's order specifying drug, strength, quantity, directions, refills and DAW; identified at the pharmacy by Rx number + fill number.
* [Prior Authorization (PA / ePA)](/prescriptions/prior-authorization.md) - Payer approval required before coverage; electronic PA uses NCPDP ePA; reject code 75 signals PA required.
* [Real-Time Prescription Benefit](/prescriptions/rtpb.md) - Patient-specific cost and coverage check at the point of prescribing.
