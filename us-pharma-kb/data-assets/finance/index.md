# Contracting & GTN Data

Contracts, chargebacks, rebates and government pricing.

# Concepts

* [Contracts & Customer Eligibility](/data-assets/finance/contracts-data.md) - Contract terms, tiers, price lists and eligible-member rosters (GPO, IDN, PBM, 340B).
* [Chargeback Data (844/849)](/data-assets/finance/edi-844-849-chargebacks.md) - Wholesaler chargeback claims and manufacturer responses per resale line.
* [Government Pricing Calculations](/data-assets/finance/government-pricing-calc.md) - Monthly/quarterly AMP, BP, ASP, 340B ceiling and FSS calculations from all eligible transactions.
* [Medicaid State Invoices (CMS-R-144)](/data-assets/finance/medicaid-state-invoice.md) - Quarterly state invoices of utilisation units × URA.
* [Rebate Utilisation Invoices](/data-assets/finance/rebate-utilization.md) - PBM/plan invoices listing claim-level or aggregated utilisation subject to rebates.
