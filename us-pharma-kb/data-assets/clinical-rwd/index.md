# Clinical & Real-World Data

EHR, lab, mortality and consumer data.

# Concepts

* [Consumer & SDOH Data](/data-assets/clinical-rwd/consumer-sdoh.md) - Socioeconomic and lifestyle attributes linked to de-identified patients.
* [EHR Data](/data-assets/clinical-rwd/ehr-data.md) - De-identified electronic health record data (problems, meds, vitals, notes-derived).
* [Lab Results Data](/data-assets/clinical-rwd/lab-data.md) - De-identified lab results with LOINC codes and values.
* [Mortality Data](/data-assets/clinical-rwd/mortality-data.md) - Death records linked by token to claims (e.g. LAAD enhanced with mortality).
