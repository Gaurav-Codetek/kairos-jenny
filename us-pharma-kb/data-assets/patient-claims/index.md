# Patient-Level Claims Data

De-identified longitudinal pharmacy, medical and lifecycle claims (LAAD, Komodo, Symphony).

# Concepts

* [Closed (Payer-Adjudicated) Claims](/data-assets/patient-claims/closed-claims-payer.md) - Medical and pharmacy claims from health plans for enrolled members, with eligibility files — complete for each member's enrolled period.
* [CMS Medicare FFS Claims (ResDAC / VRDC)](/data-assets/patient-claims/cms-medicare-claims.md) - Research-identifiable Medicare Parts A/B/D claims available under CMS data use agreements.
* [HealthVerity Marketplace Data](/data-assets/patient-claims/healthverity.md) - Tokenised, licensable claims, EHR, lab and consumer data that can be linked across suppliers.
* [IQVIA Dx / Hx (Professional & Institutional Claims)](/data-assets/patient-claims/iqvia-dx-hx.md) - Unadjudicated office (Dx) and hospital (Hx) medical claims (~2B per year).
* [IQVIA FIA (Formulary Impact Analyzer / Lifecycle Claims)](/data-assets/patient-claims/iqvia-fia.md) - Paid, rejected and reversed claims captured at the point of sale to measure payer friction.
* [IQVIA LAAD (Longitudinal Access & Adjudication Data)](/data-assets/patient-claims/iqvia-laad.md) - Patient-level dataset combining IQVIA Rx, lifecycle (paid/rejected/reversed), medical and remittance claims; includes copay card, source-of-business and new-to-brand flags.
* [IQVIA LRx (Longitudinal Prescription Claims)](/data-assets/patient-claims/iqvia-lrx.md) - Patient-level dispensed Rx claims from pharmacies, payers, software providers and clearinghouses (~4B claims/yr since 2006).
* [Komodo Claims Extracts (Mx / Rx)](/data-assets/patient-claims/komodo-claims.md) - Brand- or market-specific patient-level medical and pharmacy claims extracts delivered from the Healthcare Map.
* [Komodo Healthcare Map](/data-assets/patient-claims/komodo-healthcare-map.md) - Komodo Health's linked, de-identified patient-journey dataset combining open and closed medical, pharmacy, lab and EHR data for 330M+ US patients.
* [Open Medical (Mx/Dx) Claims](/data-assets/patient-claims/medical-claims-open.md) - De-identified 837 professional/institutional claims from clearinghouses and practice-management systems.
* [Open Pharmacy (Rx) Claims](/data-assets/patient-claims/pharmacy-claims-open.md) - De-identified NCPDP claims captured from pharmacies and switches regardless of payer; broad but not enrollment-complete.
* [Remittance (835) Data](/data-assets/patient-claims/remittance-835.md) - Payment outcomes for medical claims from 835 files: paid amounts, denials and adjustment reasons.
* [Symphony Health (ICON) Integrated Dataverse](/data-assets/patient-claims/symphony-idv.md) - Patient-level Rx and medical claims linked by token across open and closed sources.
