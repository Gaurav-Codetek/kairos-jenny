# Reference & Master Data

HCP/HCO master, affiliations, alignment, product and calendar data.

# Concepts

* [Account / Network Alignment](/data-assets/reference/account-alignment.md) - Account-level (VID-based) territory assignment that can override zip alignment for key accounts.
* [AMA Physician Masterfile](/data-assets/reference/ama-masterfile.md) - Record of US physicians incl. residents, with ME numbers, specialties and PDRP status.
* [Claims Drug Mapping (NDC → Product/Market)](/data-assets/reference/drug-mapping.md) - Lookup mapping NDC11/USC to product, molecule, form, strength and market (e.g. IQVIA claims drug mapping).
* [HCP–HCO Affiliation](/data-assets/reference/hcp-hco-affiliation.md) - Many-to-many links between HCPs and the organisations/systems where they practise, with role and primary flag.
* [IQVIA OneKey](/data-assets/reference/iqvia-onekey.md) - Validated HCP/HCO reference data with affiliations, specialties and contact details.
* [NPPES NPI File](/data-assets/reference/nppes.md) - CMS registry of all NPIs with names, addresses, taxonomy codes and entity type.
* [Calendar / Time Dimension](/data-assets/reference/time-dimension.md) - Canonical calendar with week-ending, month, quarter, trimester and rolling-window flags aligned to vendor data weeks.
* [Veeva OpenData / Network](/data-assets/reference/veeva-opendata.md) - Veeva HCP/HCO reference data and MDM hub issuing VIDs, custom keys and hierarchies.
* [Zip-to-Territory (ZTT) Alignment](/data-assets/reference/zip-to-territory.md) - Effective-dated mapping of 5-digit zip → territory → region → nation, plus account-level overrides.
