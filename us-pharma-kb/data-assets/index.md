# Data Assets (Dataset Types)

Every class of US pharma commercial dataset with grain, keys, refresh, vendors and pitfalls.

# Sections

* [Market Access & Patient Services Data](/data-assets/access/index.md) - Formulary, payer, hub, SP, copay and ePA data.
* [Clinical & Real-World Data](/data-assets/clinical-rwd/index.md) - EHR, lab, mortality and consumer data.
* [Contracting & GTN Data](/data-assets/finance/index.md) - Contracts, chargebacks, rebates and government pricing.
* [Patient-Level Claims Data](/data-assets/patient-claims/index.md) - De-identified longitudinal pharmacy, medical and lifecycle claims (LAAD, Komodo, Symphony).
* [Promotion & Engagement Data](/data-assets/promotion/index.md) - CRM calls, emails, samples, speaker programmes, Open Payments.
* [Provider-Level Claims Aggregates](/data-assets/provider-aggregates/index.md) - Claims rolled up to HCP/HCO (Komodo by provider, CMS PUFs).
* [Reference & Master Data](/data-assets/reference/index.md) - HCP/HCO master, affiliations, alignment, product and calendar data.
* [Prescription Audit Data](/data-assets/rx-audit/index.md) - Projected, prescriber- or market-level dispensed-Rx data (NPA, Xponent, PlanTrak).
* [Sales & Distribution Data](/data-assets/sales-distribution/index.md) - Shipment and resale data: ex-factory, 3PL/wholesaler 867, 852, DDD, SNR.
