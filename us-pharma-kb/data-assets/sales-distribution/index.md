# Sales & Distribution Data

Shipment and resale data: ex-factory, 3PL/wholesaler 867, 852, DDD, SNR.

# Concepts

* [852 Inventory / Product Activity](/data-assets/sales-distribution/edi-852-inventory.md) - Wholesaler distribution-centre inventory on hand, receipts and sales by product.
* [3PL 867 (Direct Sales Through a 3PL)](/data-assets/sales-distribution/edi-867-3pl.md) - 867-format sales and returns from a manufacturer's 3PL, covering orders the 3PL ships directly to wholesalers, specialty distributors or end accounts.
* [Wholesaler / Specialty Distributor 867 (Sell-Through)](/data-assets/sales-distribution/edi-867-wholesaler.md) - Downstream resale lines from a wholesaler or specialty distributor to each ship-to account (hospital, clinic, pharmacy), including returns.
* [Ex-Factory Sales (Sell-In)](/data-assets/sales-distribution/ex-factory-sales.md) - Manufacturer's own invoices to wholesalers/3PL customers — the revenue record before any downstream resale.
* [ICS 867 (Cencora ICS 3PL Sales Feed)](/data-assets/sales-distribution/ics-867.md) - The 867 sales/returns feed a manufacturer receives from Cencora's ICS when ICS is its 3PL.
* [IQVIA DDD (Outlet-Level Sales)](/data-assets/sales-distribution/iqvia-ddd.md) - Projected weekly/monthly sales from distributors to individual outlets (hospitals, clinics, pharmacies), classified by outlet type.
* [IQVIA National Sales Perspectives (NSP)](/data-assets/sales-distribution/iqvia-nsp.md) - Projected national $ and unit purchases by channel (retail, clinics, hospitals, LTC, mail, etc.).
* [SNR Sales Feed (Market × Product, with 340B Flag)](/data-assets/sales-distribution/snr-sales.md) - Weekly/monthly market- and product-level sales feed (WAC $, AWP, volume/pack/extended units) with a 340B transaction flag.
