# Definition
The 867 sales/returns feed a manufacturer receives from Cencora's ICS when ICS is its 3PL — every shipment and return to wholesalers and direct accounts, at invoice-line level.[^cencora-ics-data]

# How it is produced
ICS sends daily 867 files. Manufacturers usually land them raw, type and de-duplicate them, then master each ship-to customer to their HCO master (for example Veeva Network VIDs, matched on DEA, HIN, NPI and address) and add territory alignment before building account-level sales marts.

# Grain & keys
* **Grain:** invoice line (INVOICE_ID + INVOICE_ITEM + source/ship-to; INVOICE_ID + INVOICE_ITEM alone is often not unique)
* **Primary key:** `INVOICE_ID, INVOICE_ITEM, SOURCE` plus the ship-to
* **Join keys:** `CUST_DEA` → /identifiers/provider/dea-number.md, `CUST_HIN` → /identifiers/provider/hin.md, `NDC_CODE` → /identifiers/product/ndc.md, `CUST_ZIP` → /data-assets/reference/zip-to-territory.md, `CONTRACT_ID` → /data-assets/finance/contracts-data.md
* **Refresh:** daily files
* **Projection:** census
* **Measures:** units sold/returned, WAC, amount, gross sales
* **PHI status:** none (B2B; ship-to names, addresses, DEA and HIN are business data)

# Schema
Typical fields (vendor layouts differ; confirm against the data dictionary):

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `INVOICE_DATE, INVOICE_ID, INVOICE_ITEM` | transaction |
| `SOURCE, SOURCE_DISTRIBUTOR_NAME` | feed origin |
| `CUST_* (name, address, DEA, HIN, zip)` | ship-to as reported |
| `NDC_CODE, NDC_QUALIFIER, PRODUCT` | product |
| `QUANTITY, QUANTITY_QUALIFIER, QUANTITY_SOLD, QUANTITY_RETURNED, TOTAL_ADJUSTED_QTY_SOLD` | units |
| `WAC, WAC_BASE, AMOUNT, GROSS_SALES` | money |
| `CONTRACT_ID` | contract |

Manufacturers commonly add mastered customer keys (HCO and system IDs) and territory alignment columns downstream; those are not part of the ICS feed.

# Typical uses
* Account-level sales marts
* Territory sales and incentive compensation
* Chargeback forecasting when joined to contracts

# Pitfalls
* INVOICE_ID + INVOICE_ITEM alone is often not unique (several sources or ship-tos per line); check the grain before aggregating
* Confirm the currency/unit of WAC and AMOUNT and the meaning of QUANTITY_QUALIFIER codes with ICS before summing quantities
* When both account-based and ZIP-based territory alignments are added downstream, pick one per use case
