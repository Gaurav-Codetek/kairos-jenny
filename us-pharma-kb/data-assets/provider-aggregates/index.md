# Provider-Level Claims Aggregates

Claims rolled up to HCP/HCO (Komodo by provider, CMS PUFs).

# Concepts

* [CMS Medicare Physician & Other Practitioners PUF](/data-assets/provider-aggregates/cms-part-b-provider.md) - Public Part B services and payments by NPI and HCPCS (includes J-codes).
* [CMS Medicare Part D Prescribers PUF](/data-assets/provider-aggregates/cms-part-d-prescriber.md) - Public Medicare Part D claims and costs by prescriber NPI and drug.
* [Komodo Claims by Provider (HCP × Affiliation Aggregate)](/data-assets/provider-aggregates/komodo-claims-by-provider.md) - Komodo claims aggregated to HCP with affiliated organisation and system, rolling time windows (C3M/C6M/C12M), total vs non-hemodialysis volume and claims.
