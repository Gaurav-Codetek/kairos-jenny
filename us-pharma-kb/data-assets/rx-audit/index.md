# Prescription Audit Data

Projected, prescriber- or market-level dispensed-Rx data (NPA, Xponent, PlanTrak).

# Concepts

* [IQVIA MIDAS](/data-assets/rx-audit/iqvia-midas.md) - Global audited pharmaceutical sales across countries, used for global market sizing.
* [IQVIA NPA (National Prescription Audit)](/data-assets/rx-audit/iqvia-npa.md) - Projected national dispensed prescriptions (TRx/NRx) by product and channel.
* [IQVIA Xponent (Prescriber-Level Rx)](/data-assets/rx-audit/iqvia-xponent.md) - Projected weekly dispensed prescriptions by prescriber and product across retail, mail and LTC.
* [Symphony Health (ICON) PHAST Prescription](/data-assets/rx-audit/symphony-phast.md) - Projected prescriber- and national-level Rx from Symphony's integrated dataverse.
* [IQVIA Xponent PlanTrak](/data-assets/rx-audit/xponent-plantrak.md) - Prescriber-level Rx split by payer plan to measure access and pull-through.
