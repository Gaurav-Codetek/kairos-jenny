# Promotion & Engagement Data

CRM calls, emails, samples, speaker programmes, Open Payments.

# Concepts

* [CRM Call Activity (Veeva)](/data-assets/promotion/crm-calls.md) - Rep and MSL interactions recorded in CRM: calls, approved emails (RTE), meetings, products discussed and samples.
* [Digital & Omnichannel Engagement](/data-assets/promotion/digital-engagement.md) - HCP email opens, web/portal visits, webinars and programmatic media exposure.
* [CMS Open Payments](/data-assets/promotion/open-payments.md) - Public record of manufacturer and GPO transfers of value to physicians, NPPs and teaching hospitals.
* [Promotional Audit (Competitive)](/data-assets/promotion/promotional-audit.md) - Panel-based estimates of competitor details, samples and spend.
* [Sample Transactions](/data-assets/promotion/samples.md) - PDMA-regulated sample disbursements with lot numbers and signatures.
* [Speaker Program Data](/data-assets/promotion/speaker-programs.md) - Events, speakers, attendees and honoraria for promotional speaker programmes.
