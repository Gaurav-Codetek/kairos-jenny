# Market Access & Patient Services Data

Formulary, payer, hub, SP, copay and ePA data.

# Concepts

* [Copay Program Data](/data-assets/access/copay-program-data.md) - Claims paid by the manufacturer copay programme via its BIN/PCN.
* [ePA Transaction Data](/data-assets/access/epa-data.md) - Electronic prior authorisation requests and outcomes.
* [Formulary & Policy Data](/data-assets/access/formulary-data.md) - Plan-level coverage, tier, PA/step-therapy policy and covered lives by product.
* [Hub / Patient Services Data](/data-assets/access/hub-data.md) - Case records from manufacturer hubs: enrolments, benefit verification, PA support, financial assistance, SP triage.
* [Payer & Covered Lives](/data-assets/access/payer-lives.md) - Enrollment counts by plan, PBM, channel and geography.
* [Specialty Pharmacy Data Feed](/data-assets/access/sp-data-feed.md) - Status and dispense records from network SPs (referral received, PA pending, shipped, discontinued).
