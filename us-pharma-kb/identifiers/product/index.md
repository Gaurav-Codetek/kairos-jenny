# Product Identifiers

NDC, RxNorm, GPI, HCPCS and vendor product codes.

# Concepts

* [ATC Code](/identifiers/product/atc.md) - WHO Anatomical Therapeutic Chemical classification.
* [CMF / Product Group ID (IQVIA)](/identifiers/product/cmf.md) - IQVIA product codes (`CMF10`, `PRODUCT_GROUP_ID`) used in audit deliverables instead of NDC.
* [GCN / HICL (First Databank)](/identifiers/product/gcn.md) - First Databank formulation (GCN) and ingredient (HICL) codes.
* [GPI (Medi-Span)](/identifiers/product/gpi.md) - 14-character Generic Product Identifier hierarchy from drug group to strength.
* [HCPCS Level II (J-codes)](/identifiers/product/hcpcs.md) - CMS codes for physician-administered drugs on medical claims; billing units differ from NDC units.
* [National Drug Code (NDC)](/identifiers/product/ndc.md) - FDA three-segment product identifier (labeler-product-package) for US drugs.
* [NDC-12 (6-4-2)](/identifiers/product/ndc-12.md) - Uniform 12-digit NDC format mandated from 7 Mar 2033.
* [RxNorm RxCUI](/identifiers/product/rxnorm-rxcui.md) - NLM normalized clinical drug concept identifier, linking NDCs across labelers.
* [USC (IQVIA Uniform System of Classification)](/identifiers/product/usc.md) - IQVIA therapeutic class code used in Xponent, NPA and LAAD (`USC_CODE`).
