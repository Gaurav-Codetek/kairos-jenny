# Service Categories

What each category of provider delivers.

# Concepts

* [3PL & Specialty Logistics](/service-providers/categories/3pl-logistics.md) - Warehousing, order-to-cash, cold chain and DSCSA serialisation.
* [CDMO / CMO](/service-providers/categories/cdmo.md) - Contract development and manufacturing of APIs, drug products and biologics, fill-finish and packaging.
* [Commercial Analytics & Consulting](/service-providers/categories/commercial-analytics.md) - Targeting, alignment, sizing, IC, forecasting, launch, brand analytics, data management and BI (often with offshore delivery).
* [Commercial Data Provider](/service-providers/categories/commercial-data-provider.md) - Licenses Rx, claims, sales, reference and formulary data.
* [Compliance & Transparency Services](/service-providers/categories/compliance-transparency.md) - Open Payments aggregate spend, speaker-programme management, state reporting.
* [CRM & Commercial Software](/service-providers/categories/crm-software.md) - CRM, content/MLR, reference data and MDM platforms.
* [Contract Research Organization (CRO)](/service-providers/categories/cro.md) - Clinical trial design, site management, monitoring, data management, biostatistics and submissions.
* [Contract Sales Organization (CSO)](/service-providers/categories/cso.md) - Outsourced field sales forces and inside sales.
* [GTN, Contracting & Channel Data Management](/service-providers/categories/gtn-channel-data.md) - Contract management, chargebacks, rebates, government pricing, and aggregation of 867/852/SP feeds.
* [Hub & Patient Services Providers](/service-providers/categories/hub-patient-services.md) - Hub operations, BV/PA, copay administration, nurse educators.
* [IT & Data Platform Services](/service-providers/categories/it-services.md) - Data platforms (Databricks, Snowflake), integration, MDM implementation and AI engineering.
* [Market Access, Pricing & HEOR](/service-providers/categories/market-access-heor.md) - Value dossiers, pricing strategy, HEOR and RWE studies.
* [Medical Communications & Healthcare Marketing Agencies](/service-providers/categories/medcomms-agencies.md) - Publications, medical information, HCP/DTC creative and media.
* [Regulatory & Pharmacovigilance Outsourcing](/service-providers/categories/regulatory-pv.md) - Submission publishing, AE case processing and signal detection.
* [Tokenization & Privacy](/service-providers/categories/tokenization-privacy.md) - De-identification, patient tokens and HIPAA expert determinations for linking data.
